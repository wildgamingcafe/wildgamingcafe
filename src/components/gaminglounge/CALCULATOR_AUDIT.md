## 2. Audit Findings

### Tools That Should Be Removed
1. **Pomodoro Estimator**: Search intent is for an interactive timer, not an estimator. Low traffic, zero commercial value.
2. **Discount Calculator**: Too saturated by massive coupon sites. Low commercial intent.

### Tools That Should Be Added
1. **Mortgage Calculator**: The holy grail of commercial value. Extremely high ad RPMs and lead gen potential.
2. **Time Card / Timesheet Calculator**: Massive B2B search volume. Drives HR/Payroll software affiliate revenue. Medium SEO difficulty compared to Mortgage.
